Evenli

Trip expense splitter for Android

Enjoy the trip. Settle up later.

Evenli keeps track of what everyone paid on a trip with friends or family, then tells you who owes whom and how much. Only the person keeping track needs to install it.

    What it does

    Log each expense

    Amount, currency, who paid and who it's split between, with a note so you can tell expenses apart later.

    12 currencies, no rate lookups

    Log in the local currency as you go. Settle each currency on its own, or merge everything into the trip's currency with rates you enter yourself.

    Fewest transfers

    See what each person paid, their share and what they get back or owe. Evenli suggests a settle-up with as few transfers as possible.

    Tick off paybacks

    Mark each transfer as it's paid back and see who's left. When everyone is even, look back at the trip with your photos.

    Receipts with each expense

    Keep a receipt photo with each expense, taken with the camera or picked from your photo library.

    Share with everyone

    Send the result as an image or text to any messaging app. Your friends don't need Evenli.

    Before you download

    Price
    Free, with ads. An optional one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks every animal avatar, plus your own photos as avatars.
    Works on
    Android phones
    Your data
    Trips stay on your device. No account needed, and it works offline.
    Good to know
    You enter exchange rates yourself. Evenli only does the maths for shared costs; it doesn't send or receive money.
