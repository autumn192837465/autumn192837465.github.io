About

I’m a Tokyo-based Unity Engineer with more than five years of experience building mobile games across both in-game and out-game systems. I started teaching myself Unity while studying at university.

At Rudel, I worked on the development and live operation of three released titles. I later served as Lead Engineer, establishing reusable UI and scene-management foundations, coding standards, and templates for a new project. I now focus on core gameplay development for Puzzle Panic at Limit Break.

Outside work, I build games independently. Planet Drop is available on Google Play and the App Store, while Pattern Hero is currently in development. I handle the full process myself, from planning and programming through store submission and release.

I collaborate with international teams in Chinese, Japanese, and English.
