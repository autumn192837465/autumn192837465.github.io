About

I’m a senior Unity engineer with more than five years of mobile game development in Tokyo, on four titles that have launched.

My focus is gameplay and boss systems, out-game UI and live events, backed by async code with UniTask, unit tests and debug tools that keep a live game easy to update. At Rudel I worked on three live titles and became lead engineer. At Limit Break I’m a senior client engineer on Puzzle Panic, working every day with designers, planners and QA in Japan, the US and the Philippines.

Outside work I also make apps independently, handling planning, development, release and promotion myself. Four are live on the App Store and Google Play.
